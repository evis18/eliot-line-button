Basic Strategy Card

Hard totals

17+: Stand. 13-16 stand vs 2-6, otherwise hit. 12 stands vs 4-6. 11 doubles all. 10 doubles vs 2-9. 9 doubles vs 3-6.

Soft totals

A,8 stands except double vs 6. A,7 doubles vs 2-6, stands vs 7-8, hits vs 9-A. A,2-A,6 double in strong dealer-bust spots.

Pairs

Always split A,A and 8,8. Never split 10s or 5s. Split 2s/3s/6s/7s in dealer weak spots. Split 9s except vs 7, 10, A.

Surrender note

Some Wynn tables offer late surrender, but this trainer uses only Hit, Stand, Double, Split. Surrender spots use the playable fallback.
